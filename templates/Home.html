Biography

Dr. Dharmendra Sharma joined NMIMS Indore Campus in May 2018 as Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, within the School of Technology Management and Engineering.

He earned his doctorate in Computer Science and Engineering from MU, Chittorgarh, in 2016. His doctoral research examined the role of term weighting in the performance of Information Storage and Retrieval systems, and he has further contributed to query knowledge enhancement and duplicate content sharing for Information Retrieval.

He has participated in workshops and faculty development programmes organised by IIT Bombay, NPTEL, edX and Coursera. As Faculty Coordinator of the Turing Club, he has led initiatives including the data-focused hackathon Datathon 2021, the Arcania Quiz series, and a credit course on Agile Software Development in collaboration with TCS.
